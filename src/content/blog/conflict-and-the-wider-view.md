Some conflicts end with an agreement and leave nothing behind. Others end with an agreement and leave everything behind: the hurt, the distance, the sense that something important was never touched.

The seventh level of the Seven Levels Framework, Spirituality, speaks to that second kind.

## What we mean by Spirituality

Here, Spirituality is not about a particular belief or tradition. It is about meaning. The values we live by. Our sense of connection to others and to something larger than ourselves. The question of who we are becoming.

People of every faith, and of none, bring this level with them into conflict, whether or not they name it.

## When agreement is not enough

We might notice this level when a conflict touches questions of fairness, forgiveness or repair. When "we have settled it" is true on paper, and something still feels unresolved.

In those moments, we might ask:

- Which of my deepest values does this conflict touch?
- What might this conflict be inviting me to learn, or to release?
- What would peace look like here, beyond simply ending the conflict?

## A wider view

One practice we return to often is simple. We imagine looking back on this conflict ten years from now. What would we hope to have learned? How would we hope to have treated the people involved?

The answers rarely tell us exactly what to do. They often tell us *how* we would like to do it, and that can be enough to take the next step.
